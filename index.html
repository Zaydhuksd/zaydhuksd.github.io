Cramps aren't random — they're chemical.

During your period, your uterine lining releases prostaglandins, which make the uterus contract to shed tissue. The stronger the contraction, the more it can cramp. Heat works because it relaxes that muscle and improves blood flow to the area.

How it works

One reusable heat core, a fresh pad every time. The core is the only part you keep — the pad itself is disposable, just like a regular pad.

1

Microwave the core

60–90 seconds is usually enough. The natural filling — rice, wheat, flaxseed, and cherry pit — holds heat evenly.

2

Slot it into the pad

Every pad has a built-in pocket positioned right over the uterus, so the core sits exactly where you need it.

3

Wear it, then reheat

You'll get roughly 30–40 minutes of steady warmth. Reheat the same core for your next pad — it's built to outlast any single pack.

Heat, done safely

The heat core is designed to stay in a comfortable range, not a scalding one — but microwaves vary, so a few habits matter.

  • Start with 60 seconds, check warmth, then add time in 15-second steps if needed.
  • Always test the core against your wrist before placing it against skin.
  • Let it cool fully before reheating — don't stack heat on top of residual heat.
  • Not intended for unsupervised overnight use.

Questions

Is the whole pad reusable?

No — the pad itself is disposable, like a normal pad. Only the heat core inside the pocket is reusable; you keep reheating that same core pack after pack.

How long does one heat core last?

The core is built to be reheated hundreds of times before the filling loses its ability to hold warmth evenly.

Can I use it if I have sensitive skin?

The top layer is cotton and the backing avoids petroleum-based plastic, which tends to be gentler than standard synthetic pads — but as with any new product, we'd suggest a short trial period first.
